# LeadDash Employees

A guide for recording demo videos, training videos and written SOPs of the LeadDash Employees web app. Every label in quotes below is the exact text on the screen, checked in the app's code on Oct 12, 2026. Where a label includes a number or a name, it is shown as `<Name>` or `(N)`.

LeadDash Employees gives a business owner a team of AI employees. Each employee has one job, a chat, a Work tab with the work they make, and settings the owner controls. Everything an employee writes that would go out to the world waits for a person to approve it.

## Where and how

### Web address

- The app lives at **https://employees.leaddash.io**.
- The sign-in screen is at **https://employees.leaddash.io/signin**. Any page you open while signed out shows the same sign-in screen.
- The sign-in screen has three links under the box: "About", "Privacy" and "Terms". These open public pages and need no sign-in.

### How to sign in

1. Type your email in the "Email" box and press "Send code".
2. The screen changes to "Enter your code" and shows "Sent to <your email>".
3. Open that inbox, find the 6-digit code and type it in the "6-digit code" box.
4. Press "Sign in". The "Sign in" button stays gray until all 6 digits are typed.
5. "Use a different email" takes you back to step 1.

Codes last 10 minutes. After 5 wrong tries the code locks and you start again.

### Which workspace to record in

- A workspace is one business's copy of the app: its employees, Brain, Projects, chats and settings.
- Record only in **LeadDash's own workspace** or a **demo workspace** that LeadDash staff made for recording. Never record in a customer's workspace.
- The current workspace name shows at the top of the Chats list. Click it (or the LeadDash Employees logo at the top of the left rail) to open the workspace switcher. The current workspace has a check mark. Pick the demo workspace there.
- If the switcher shows a "New workspace" row, you are signed in with a LeadDash staff account. Do not press it.

### What makes a good demo setup

Check these before you press record:

- **Generic business facts only.** The Brain, Workspace page and Knowledge files hold a made-up business with a generic name. No real clients, no real patients, no health information, no real customer names.
- **Several employees already on the team and onboarded.** Bringing someone on and onboarding them takes time. Have a few done so their chats and Work tabs have work to show, and leave a few on the bench so you can record "Bring on".
- **Sample work in place.** A few drafts in Approvals, a few posts on Sienna's calendar, a video or two on Elena's Videos tab, a project in Projects, a past meeting with notes.
- **Outside accounts left disconnected, or connected only to test accounts LeadDash owns.** When a channel (Gmail, LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, X and others) is not connected, pressing "Approve" does not send anything. The item waits with the pill "Waiting on channel". That is the safest way to show approvals.
- **Know whether the workspace has a plan.** A workspace with a plan shows a work hours bar and asks "Go ahead" or "Not now" before big jobs. LeadDash's own workspaces have no plan: work is counted but not limited, and that question does not appear. Pick the workspace that matches what you want to show. Do not change the plan to get it.
- **Healthcare or not.** A workspace set as "Healthcare practice" shows extra screens (LeadDash EHR connection, Client names switch, Harper and Camille). A "Business" or "Nonprofit" workspace does not. Record in the type that fits your video.
- **A staff account shows extra things.** If you are signed in as LeadDash staff, some screens show staff-only cards (for example "Base instructions" and "App review access" on My account, and extra details on voices). Keep those out of any recording. A regular owner or admin login on the demo workspace is better for recording.
- **Browser.** Use Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer at full width. On a phone-width window the left rail disappears and a bottom bar takes its place.

## The employees

### How every employee's page works

Open "Chats" and click an employee in the list. Their page has a header and tabs.

**The header** shows their photo, name and job title, then the tabs, then a status pill: "Ready", "Working" or "On the bench". Owners and admins also see two buttons: "Vacation" and "Terminate". In a healthcare workspace, employees who work with client information also show a blue "Client info" pill and a "Client names" switch with "Show", "Initial" and "Hide". That switch changes how client names show on your own screen right away.

**The tabs**, in order:

| Tab | Path | What is on it |
|---|---|---|
| "Chat" | /chats/<kind> | Talk to the employee and give them work. |
| The Work tab (its name changes by job) | /chats/<kind>/work | The work this employee made. |
| "B-roll" (Elena only) | /chats/video/broll | Elena's library of video clips. |
| "Knowledge" | /chats/<kind>/knowledge | Files, links and text only this employee reads. |
| "Onboarding" | /chats/<kind>/onboarding | How the employee will work, their photo and voice, and scheduled assignments. |
| "Guidelines" | /chats/<kind>/guidelines | Their name, the rules they follow, and rules that never change. |

**The Chat tab**
- Messages show the sender, the time and a "Reply" link. Days are split by a date line such as "Today, Oct 12, 2026".
- When the chat is empty it shows the employee's job description.
- Some messages carry cards with their own buttons (a draft post, a video, a plan).
- Employees who search the web show "Searches <Name> ran (N)" under their answer. Click it to see the searches.
- While an employee works, three dots show with "<Name> is working". Morgan, Taylor and Elena also show "Searching the web can take a minute or two."
- Above the message box sit suggestion chips. Clicking one sends it right away.
- The message box says "Message <Name>". It has a paperclip for "Attach files", an emoji button, an "@" button to mention someone, a green "Talk" button for a voice one-on-one, and "Send". Enter sends. Shift+Enter makes a new line.
- Typing "@" lists people and other AI employees. A tagged employee gets the message in their own chat. A tagged person gets a notice.

**The Knowledge tab**
- "Add" at the top right opens "Add to <Name>'s knowledge" with three choices: "File", "Link" and "Text". Then "Title", "Folder", and "Save" or "Cancel".
- Files can be PDF (scanned too), Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text.
- Folder tabs across the top: "All (N)" then the folders. Morgan has "Past applications", "Boilerplate", "Budgets", "Attachments" and "RFPs". Taylor has "Talks and bios", "Past proposals" and "Attachments". Everyone else has "Examples", "Notes" and "Attachments". Folders the employee fills in on their own ("Reviewer comments", "Answers") show only once they hold something.
- Each row shows Item, Kind, Read and Added, with "Open". Clicking a row opens it in place below with "Sections found" or "What it says", "Used in" and "Folder", plus "Edit" and "Remove".

**The Onboarding tab**, top to bottom:
1. **"How <Name> will work"**. The employee drafts this from the Brain. A pill says "Drafted from your Brain, check it" until you approve it, then "Approved <date>". Rows marked "Guess, check this" are guesses. Buttons: "Edit" (then "Save" and "Cancel"), "Looks right" ("<Name> starts working from this the moment you press it."), and after approval "Redo from the Brain". A link at the bottom says "Answer the full interview instead (N parts, about 10 minutes)" or, once done, "See the full interview".
2. **A job card** for some employees: Simone's "Meetings", Nora's "Projects", Avery's "Notetaker", Malik's lead setup, Riley's search, Jada's "LinkedIn step".
3. **"Works on <his or her> own"**, what they do without asking, and "Always asks you".
4. **"A day with <Name>"**, a typical day, with "Rewrite".
5. **"Photo and voice"**, with "Play" to hear the voice and "Edit" to change the photo or voice.
6. **"Assignments"**, scheduled work, with "Add assignment".

**The Guidelines tab**, top to bottom:
1. **"Employee"** card: "Name", "Job" and "Works for", with "Edit". Only the name can change.
2. If two rules disagree, an amber card says "<Name> found guidelines that disagree" with a button for each way to settle it and "Not a conflict".
3. A card with "Learn" and "Check". For writing jobs it is called "Learn from your examples". Otherwise it is "Check for conflicts".
4. The guideline sections for this job, each numbered, each line marked where it came from ("From your interview", "Added in chat, <date>", "Added by you, <date>" and others), each section with its own "Edit".
5. **"Always followed"**, fixed rules for this job that you cannot change.

### Who is on the team

Which employees a workspace has depends on its organization type:
- **Business** and **Nonprofit** workspaces do not have Harper or Camille.
- **Healthcare practice** workspaces do not have Riley, Jada or Kai.
- **Kai and Zara** are only in LeadDash's own workspaces, never in a customer's.
- In a healthcare practice, Malik's title is "Intake Coordinator" and Imani's is "Clinician Onboarding Specialist".
- The Chats list groups employees by department. Business and nonprofit: Leadership, Revenue, Sales, Marketing, Operations. Healthcare: Leadership, Client care, Billing and compliance, Growth, Operations.
- A workspace can rename any employee on their Guidelines tab. The job stays the same.

### Bench interview

For talking with an employee before you bring them on. Path: /bench/<kind>. Owners and admins only.

- Left side: "Back to bench", the photo with "Speaking", "Thinking", "Voice off" or "Listening", the name and role, what they do, and "Bring on <Name>" and "Mute voice" (or "Voice on").
- Main area: "Interview <Name> to come off the bench", and a counter "<N> answers · <M> min of work hours". Each answer uses 5 minutes of work hours.
- The employee speaks first. Chips: "What do you need from me?", "Show me an example", "What does this cost in hours?"

- For: A business owner or assistant
- Starts on: Avery's "Desk" tab (/chats/inbox/work), "Drafts"
- Needs in the demo: A made-up email with no real names or client details
- Steps:
  1. Under "Paste a message", fill "From", "Subject" and "Message".
  2. Press "Draft reply". It says "Drafting...", then a row shows under "Email drafts (N)".
  3. Click the row. It opens with "What they want" and "Draft reply".
  4. Press "Edit", change the reply, and press "Save".
  5. Press "Send back" to show how to return it, or "Dismiss" to drop it.
- Done when: The draft shows in "Email drafts (N)" with its urgency.
- Watch out: "Approve" sends the reply from Gmail when Google is connected. Do not approve in a recording unless Google is disconnected.

### Add a calendar hold
- For: A business owner or assistant
- Starts on: Avery's "Desk" tab, "Drafts", then "Calendar holds (N)"
- Needs in the demo: Google disconnected or connected only to a test calendar
- Steps:
  1. Under "Add a hold", fill "Title", "Date" (MM/DD/YYYY), "Time" (like 2:00 PM), "Attendees" and "Agenda".
  2. Press "Add hold". A row shows under "Calendar holds (N)".
  3. Click the row to see "Details".
- Done when: The hold shows in the list with its date and time.
- Watch out: "Approve" puts it on the real calendar if Google is connected. Use only made-up attendees.

### Make a decision on Avery's Desk
- For: A business owner
- Starts on: Avery's "Desk" tab, "Today"
- Needs in the demo: At least one open decision
- Steps:
  1. Read "Your top three, <date>".
  2. Press "Decide" on an item. The "Decisions" tab opens with it open.
  3. Read the options. "Avery suggests" marks his pick.
  4. Pick an option and press "Approve <option>". Or press "Add note", "Send back" or "Later".

- Watch out: Some decisions say only the owner can decide. Others can add a note.

### Meetings and notes

### Run a meeting with Simone
- For: A business owner or manager
- Starts on: Simone's "Meetings" tab (/chats/coo/work)
- Needs in the demo: A repeating meeting set on Simone's Onboarding tab, with made-up attendees
- Steps:
  1. On "Upcoming (N)", click a meeting. It opens with "Agenda", "Link", "Invites" and "Who".
  2. If there is no agenda, press "Write agenda".
  3. Press "Edit" to change the time, length, attendees or agenda items, then "Save".
  4. After the meeting, go to "Past (N)" and open it.
  5. Paste the notes in "Your notes" and press "Save notes". "Action items Simone found" fills in.
  6. Press "Send to Nora". The items show "With Nora" or "In Projects".
- Done when: The past meeting shows its action items with their status.
- Watch out: "Send invite" and "Send recap" email the attendees for real. Do not press them in a demo unless every attendee is a test address. "Cancel meeting" cancels it.

### Hold a team huddle
- For: A business owner
- Starts on: Huddle (/huddle)
- Needs in the demo: A microphone and a few employees on the team
- Steps:
  1. Under "Who's in", pick "Whole team", "Departments" or "Pick people".
  2. Press "Start the huddle". The tiles and "Transcript" show.
  3. Press "Start talking" and allow the microphone.
  4. Say a name and a question, like "Simone, what is on the agenda this week?". That employee answers out loud.
  5. Press "End". The notes go to Simone and the action items to Nora.
- Done when: The page shows "Last huddle" with "Open notes".
- Watch out: Do not paste a real meeting link into "Bring the team". Only employees on the team join.

## Words the app uses

- **Workspace**: one business's copy of the app, with its own team, Brain, Projects and settings. Switch with the logo at the top of the rail.
- **AI employee**: one of the roster (Simone, Nora, Morgan and the rest). Each has one job.
- **The bench**: where employees wait until the owner brings them on. Bios are free. Interviews use work hours.
- **Bring on**: move an employee from the bench to the team. They join Chats and offer to start onboarding.
- **Onboarding**: the employee's plan for the job, drafted from the Brain ("How <Name> will work"), approved with "Looks right".
- **Guidelines**: the rules an employee follows, from onboarding, chat and you. "Always followed" are fixed rules.
- **The Brain**: facts every employee reads on every job.
- **Knowledge**: files, links and text only one employee reads.
- **Approvals**: the queue of everything that waits for a person before it goes out.
- **Send back**: return a draft to the employee to change.
- **Waiting on channel**: approved, but the account it goes out through is not connected, so nothing was sent.
- **Work hours**: units of work, not clock time. Each finished job uses a fixed amount. Chat is free. A job that fails uses nothing.
- **Plan**: how many work hours a month the workspace has. Hours reset on the 1st of each month.
- **Hour packs** ("Add hours"): extra hours bought on top of a plan. They last 12 months.
- **Work-hour limits**: how many hours each person in the workspace can use each month.
- **Hours saved**: the total at the bottom of the Chats list, an estimate of time the team saved this month.
- **Assignment**: scheduled work an employee does on its own, on a repeat.
- **Handoff**: one employee passing work to another, shown in chat and Activity.
- **One-on-one**: a voice conversation with one employee, started with "Talk".
- **Huddle**: a voice conversation with several employees at once.
- **Desk**: Avery's queue of decisions, waiting items, drafts and rules.
- **Hold**: a calendar block Avery drafts for approval.
- **Sitting in**: Avery joining a Zoom, Google Meet or Teams meeting to take notes.
- **Website login**: a saved sign-in the employees use in their own browser. Passwords are never shown to the AI.
- **Sub-account lock** ("Only this sub-account"): keeps a login to one sub-account of a site.
- **B-roll**: short video clips Elena reuses across videos.
- **Style**: the look of a video (real footage or a drawn style).
- **Formats**: video structures that work now, which Elena can recreate.
- **SOP**: a written step-by-step procedure, in the Handbook. Statuses: "Draft", "Writing", "In review", "Current", "Retired", "Review due".
- **Client info** and **Client names**: in a healthcare practice, the pill on employees who work with client information, and the switch ("Show", "Initial", "Hide") for how client names show on your screen.
- **Vacation**: an onboarded employee off the team for at least 30 days, with no work hours used.
- **Terminated**: off the team, stored, rehirable after 90 days.
- **Team chat only**: a role that sees channels, direct messages, the Team page and their account, and no AI employees.

- Never press "Send invite" on the Team page or on a meeting, "Send recap", "Send now" on a nudge, or "Bring the team" with a real meeting link.

- Never record a password being typed during "Record my screen", and never record a real chart in LeadDash EHR. Use the demo practice.
