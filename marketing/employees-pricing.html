How hours work

  • A work hour is a unit of work, not time on a clock. Each job uses the amount in the table, every time.
  • Hours are shared by the whole team and reset on the 1st of each month. Unused plan hours don't roll over.
  • Hours you buy are used after your plan's hours and last 12 months.
  • Jobs that use 3 hours or more ask you first.
  • If an employee can't finish a job, it uses no hours.
  • At 0 hours, employees pause until you add hours or your plan resets. Chat still works.
