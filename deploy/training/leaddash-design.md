### Fonts (Google Fonts)

- Headlines: Playfair Display, with one emphasized word per headline in italic, in gold or orange.
- Labels, buttons and navigation: Sora (section labels 11px, bold, uppercase, wide letter spacing).
- Body: DM Sans, 17px, line height 1.7.

### Layout rhythm

- Alternate dark forest sections and cream sections down the page.
- Section spacing about 96px on phones and 128px on desktop; content width about 1080px.
- Pill buttons (fully rounded) in orange with white text, lifting slightly on hover.
- Cards: white on cream, or emerald and forest on dark, rounded 16 to 20px, soft shadows.
- A subtle grain texture on dark sections is part of the look; keep it light.
- Section labels (eyebrows) above headlines in Sora, emerald on cream and gold on dark.

### Logo and marks

- Wordmark: "LEAD" and "DASH", never placed directly on brand green without a dark or light backing.
- The HIPAA seal and the founder seal use gold or orange rings on dark green.

### Content rules for pages

- The 24/7 receptionist is the lead proof feature on platform pages; never call it an AI receptionist.
- Real media credits only (TLC, PBS, NPR, FOX, CBS and others on file). No invented testimonials or customer logos.
- Dr. Ashley's photos from the Brain for founder and about sections.
