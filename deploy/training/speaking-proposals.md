### Session title

The Relational Skills HR Already Has, and Almost Never Uses on Themselves

### Summary (75 words or less, as written)

HR professionals are among the most skilled relationship managers in any organization. They know how to praise, understand, listen, self-regulate, and empathize, with everyone but themselves. This session uses the P.U.L.S.E. Framework®, a federally registered relational attunement model, to help HR leaders redirect their own strongest skills inward, preventing burnout, building resilience, and creating the sustainable professional practice needed to keep showing up for the organizations they serve.

### Learning objectives (written for HRCI credit)

1. Identify the specific ways HR professionals deplete their own relational resources by directing empathy, listening, and self-regulation outward without replenishment, and recognize the behavioral and organizational warning signs of unsustainable professional practice.
2. Apply the P.U.L.S.E. Framework® (Praise, Understand, Listen, Self-Regulate, Empathize) as a structured self-assessment tool to identify the highest-leverage personal and organizational intervention points for preventing HR burnout and attrition.
3. Develop a concrete, personalized sustainability action plan that brings the P.U.L.S.E. Framework® into daily practice, team leadership and culture strategy, with at least two actions they can start within 30 days.

## [speaking] SHRM Arkansas audience research

What the March 2026 research found about this conference, so any follow-up, slides or pitch fit the room.

- About 450 HR professionals from across Arkansas: HR directors, CHROs, HR managers and business executives.
- A regional conference with a practical, action-oriented culture. Sessions lean toward real-world tools, not theory.
- Sessions earn SHRM PDC and HRCI credits, so content has to map to HR competencies.
- In 2025 they chose "HR for HR" sessions about HR's own burnout, such as the keynote "Putting Your Mask on First: HR for HR" and "Creative Balance: A Toolkit for Sustainable HR Leadership."
- They choose sessions built on branded frameworks with practical tools, such as "5 Levels of Leadership" and "ABCs of Wholeness." A federally registered framework stands out here.
- Workplace mental health was already covered in 2025 ("Breaking the Stigma: Transforming Workplace Mental Health into a Strategic Advantage"), so her session takes a different angle: HR's own burnout, using skills they already have.
